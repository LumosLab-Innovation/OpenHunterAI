01 / The assessment workflow

A clear path
from scope to evidence.

One engine for the dashboard and CLI. A declared public target, a bounded plan and a report that makes its limits visible.

  1. 01

    Declare the scope

    Choose the exact public HTTPS host and paths you may test. Confirm authority and select Quick, Standard or Deep.

    Authorization before execution
  2. 02

    Review the plan

    Check worker readiness and resource limits. Missing tools become explicit coverage gaps that require review.

    No silent profile downgrade
  3. 03

    Inspect the evidence

    Follow scoped observations and review candidates separately from validated findings. Sensitive actions require approval.

    A signal starts a question
  4. 04

    Keep a useful record

    Export the sanitized assessment, verification criteria and limitations. Prepare a manual handoff to OpenShieldAI.

    One immutable report snapshot

02 / AI with a defined job

Reason about risk.
Stay grounded in facts.

Models help decide which questions deserve attention. The engine keeps authority, execution and evidence under explicit controls.

Explore reasoning routes ↗
OBSERVE

Tools collect signals.

Browser inspection, passive surface checks, ZAP and curated Nuclei adapters provide observations. Their availability is checked for each run.

REASON

AI connects the context.

Summarize and deduplicate observations, rank surfaces, propose bounded hypotheses and validation plans, then explain verified facts.

CONTROL

Policy sets the boundary.

Scope, approvals, tool access and resource budgets constrain execution. Models cannot grant permission or manufacture evidence.

VERIFY

Proof earns the verdict.

Independent replay and matched controls remain release gates. A worker claim or model agreement alone cannot establish a vulnerability.

03 / A focused role in the security loop

Assessment meets
response.

Two distinct responsibilities, connected by a reviewable evidence record. Each product keeps its own authority and action controls.

OpenHunterAI / Assessment

Find. Explain. Verify.

Attack-method research, authorized testing, sanitized evidence, finding validation and finding-scoped manual retest.

Produces the assessment record
→

OpenShieldAI / Response

Investigate. Act. Recover.

Situation investigation, incident context, human-controlled containment, remediation coordination and recovery.

Explore OpenShieldAI ↗

Development handoff: a manually transferred JSON file. The snapshot includes scope, finding and validation context, sanitized evidence references, coverage and verification criteria. A digest protects snapshot integrity; downstream asset mapping and authorization remain independent. The OpenShield importer is not implemented.

Current evidence boundary

Know what has been demonstrated.

  • Worker output stays a signal or candidate until an independent validation decision exists.
  • Independent replay and positive / negative efficacy benchmarks are unfinished release gates.
  • Manual retest without a supported replay executor returns RETEST_UNSUPPORTED.
  • Missing workers, failed checks and budget stops remain visible. Zero validated findings does not establish a secure target.
